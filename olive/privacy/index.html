Olive Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Olive is a private, personal assistant app built and used only by its developer, Zhong Xuan Khwa (the "developer"). It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This policy describes what the app does with Google user data.

What data the app accesses

With the developer's consent, Olive accesses the developer's own Google Calendar through these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: view and edit events on the developer's calendars;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly: see the list of the developer's calendars.

Event data can include titles, times, locations, descriptions and the names or addresses of guests. The app does not access Gmail, contacts, Drive files, or calendar sharing and settings.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the developer: summarising the developer's schedule, finding clashes and free time, and adding or changing calendar events when the developer asks. Events that other people created are read but not changed, deleted or shared by the app unless the developer approves that action.

Where the data goes

  • The app runs on a private server owned by the developer. Google sign-in credentials are stored there, in a file readable only by the app's own user account.
  • To produce summaries and answers, the relevant calendar text is sent to the AI model providers that power the assistant (currently Anthropic and OpenAI), solely to generate a response for the developer.
  • Summaries may be delivered to the developer through the Telegram messaging app and may be kept in the assistant's own chat history and memory on the developer's server.

The data is not sold, not shared with advertisers or data brokers, not used for advertising, and not used to build or train general-purpose AI models. No person other than the developer reads it.

Retention and deletion

Calendar data is fetched when needed and is not copied into any separate database. Copies that appear in assistant chat history or memory stay on the developer's server until the developer deletes them. The developer can stop all access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the app's access, and can delete the stored credentials from the server.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Olive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact
